Robust Guidance Law Design of Launch Vehicles via 
Deep Reinforcement Learning Algorithm 
Chenhui Jia 1, Xiaodong Liu 1, Xiaomin Li1, Qinghai Gong 1, Zhaolei Wang 1, 
Ran Li1

Automatic Control Institute, Beijing, China

Abstract. The design of guidance laws for launch vehicles in the gale area ne-
cessitates a delicate balance between achieving high control accuracy, ensuring 
robustness against significant disturbances, and minimizing overload to safe 
guard against potential damage to the launch vehicle ’s structure. In this paper, 
we develop network formed adaptive guidance laws for various flight tasks, 
which are trained with a reinforcement learning algorithm to address the chal-
lenges mention above. The simulation results demonstrate that the guidance 
laws trained with our algorithm exhibit robust characteristics under the influ-
ence of various wind disturbances and accurately track the desired flight trajec-
tory with high precision throughout the flight process . Furthermore, in the gale 
area, the guidance laws enable the launch vehicle to operate with reduced over-
load while maintaining an acceptable level of tracking accuracy . This algorithm 
represents a promising candidate for designing guidance laws for launch vehi-
cles in the gale area or under unknown disturbances during the flight process. 
Keywords: Adaptive Guidance Law, Reinforcement Learning, Soft Actor-
Critic, Overload Reduction, Launch Vehicles. 
1 Introduction 
Launch vehicle flight control design, as a pivotal technology in the control systems of 
launch vehicles, has garnered extensive attention across the aerospace field  [1-3]. 
During the flight control process of the ascending phase after the launch of the launch 
vehicle, the engineers not only need to consider the influence of wind interference but 
also need to consider the importance of reducing the overload of the launch vehicle in 
the gale area. Due to the structural characteristics of the launch vehicle wi th large 
slenderness ratio, it is necessary to reduce the overload of the launch vehicle to a safe 
range to prevent the launch vehicle from the damage of the vehicle body. As the over-
load reduction may lead to the loss of the orbit injection accuracy during the flight 
process, especially under the disturbance of the wind in the gale area, we may consid-
er the guidance of the launch vehicle as a trade -off between the control accuracy and 
the overload reduction. Therefore an adaptive guidance law is required w hich not can 
only control the launch vehicle under the condition of unknown high disturbance, but

also balance the control accuracy and the overload reduction to make the flight pro-
cess of the launch vehicle achieves a satisfied flight process. 
As a new intelligent planning and decision algorithm, deep reinforcement learning 
algorithm (DRL algorithm for short) which combines the traditional reinforcement 
learning decision -making algorithm and the deep learning algorithm, has greatly en-
hanced the decision -making ability of agents through deep neural networks. In recent 
years, deep reinforcement learning has achieved great achievements on chess games 
[4], complex RTS computer games [5], multi -agent games [6], robots control [7], and 
autopilots [8].  
Among variety of the DRL algorithms, Soft Actor -Critic algorithm [9 -10] as an 
off-policy stochastic policy gradient algorithm [11] which is utilized to solve the 
problems of continuous action space problems. Compared with the deterministic poli-
cy gradient algorithm, such as DDPG [12] or TD3 [13], SAC algorithm demonstrates 
its more powerful exploration ability which encourages the agent to explorer new 
policies while keeping the rewards not significantly reduced. Besides, SAC algorithm 
inherits the experience replay mechanism of the off -policy DRL algorithm and the 
clipped double Q learning mechanism of TD3 algorithm so that the algorithm will 
realize the fast convergence during the training process without the overestimation of 
the accumulate returns. Due to the characteristics above, SAC algorithm is considered 
as a suitable method to solve the problem s of continuous action space with complex 
reward. In this paper, we train the guidance laws of launch vehicle with SAC algo-
rithm to solve the desired trajectory tracking problem in different disturbances of 
wind field and the overload relief problem during the flight process after launch.  
The rest of this paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we will build the math-
ematic model of a launch vehicle and construct Markov decision process of two flight 
task. In Section 3, we will introduce the design of guidance law training agent basing 
on SAC algorithm. In Section 4, we will carry out simulations of the two flight tasks 
with the guidance laws designed with our algorithm. We will draw a conclusion in 
Section 5.

5 Conclusion 
In this paper, SAC reinforcement learning algorithm is utilized to train neural -
networked guidance l aws of for two launch vehicle flight tasks.  According to the 
simulation result, with the effect of our guidance law, the launch vehicle tracks the 
desired trajectory with a satisfied tracking error during the flight process under differ-
ent disturbance of wind fields  in Task 1, moreover,  the launch vehicle can still accu-
